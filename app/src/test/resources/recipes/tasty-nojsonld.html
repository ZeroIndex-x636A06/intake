Lemon Herb Salmon

Yield: 4 servings Total Time: 25 minutes

Ingredients

  • 4 fillets salmon
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 lemon, sliced
  • 1 tbsp chopped dill

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 400F.
  2. Oil the salmon, top with lemon and dill, and roast 12 minutes.

Nutrition

  • Serving Size: 1 fillet
  • Calories: 310
  • Protein: 34 g
  • Carbohydrates: 2 g
  • Fat: 18 g
