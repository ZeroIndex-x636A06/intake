Grandma's Beef Stew

My grandmother made this every Sunday. Serves 6 and freezes well.

Ingredients

For the stew

  • 2 lb beef chuck, cubed
  • 4 carrots, sliced
  • 3 potatoes, diced

For the gravy

  • 2 tbsp flour
  • 2 cups beef broth

Instructions

  1. Brown the beef in a large pot.
  2. Add the vegetables and broth and simmer for two hours.

Notes

  • Keeps for four days in the fridge.
