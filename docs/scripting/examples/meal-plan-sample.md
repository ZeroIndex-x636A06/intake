# Meal plan: Sample Two-Week Plan

<!-- A generic SAMPLE. Not anyone's real plan, and not medical or dietary advice. -->

avoid: dairy
About:
This is a sample plan with two weeks, to show the format.
Cook one main dish on Sunday and eat it through the week.

Standing breakfasts and snacks stay the same all quarter.

## Every week
Breakfasts:
- Two boiled eggs, a banana and a handful of almonds
- Oatmeal made with water, topped with berries
Snacks:
- Apple with peanut butter
- Beef jerky
Pantry:
- rice, olive oil, soy sauce
- sea salt
Watch for:
- Seasoning packets
- Flour tortillas and bread rolls
- Breadcrumbs and crackers

## Week 1: Chicken and rice bowls
Protein: chicken
Servings: 4
Time: 35 min

Ingredients:
- 600 g chicken thigh, boneless
  per 100g: 180 kcal, 20 g protein, 0 g carbs, 11 g fat
- 300 g basmati rice, dry
  per 100g: 350 kcal, 8 g protein, 78 g carbs, 1 g fat
- 2 cups cucumber, sliced
  weight: 240 g
  per 100g: 15 kcal, 0.7 g protein, 3.6 g carbs, 0.1 g fat
- 1 lemon
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1/2 cup chopped parsley
- salt and pepper

Steps:
1. Rinse the rice until the water runs clear, then cook it, about 15 minutes.
2. Season the chicken with salt, pepper and lemon zest.
3. Sear the chicken 5 minutes per side, then rest it for 5 minutes and slice.
4. Build four bowls with rice, chicken and cucumber.

Serve title: The bowls
Serve:
Rice, chicken, cucumber and a squeeze of lemon, in a container.
Add the parsley fresh each day.

Variations:
- Chicken in a warm wrap
- Cold over greens with extra lemon

Shopping:
- Produce: 2 lemons, 1 bunch parsley, cucumbers
- Meat: 1.5 lb chicken thigh
- Pantry: basmati rice

Notes:
Rice keeps four days in the fridge. Reheat it until steaming.

## Week 2: Beef chili
Protein: beef
Servings: 8
Time: 1 hour 30 minutes

Ingredients:
- 2 lb ground beef 90/10
- 1 onion, diced
- 3 cloves garlic
- 2 cans diced tomatoes
- 2 cans kidney beans
- 3 tbsp chili powder
- 1 tbsp cumin
- beef broth
- salt

Steps:
1. Brown the beef in a large pot with the onion, about 10 min.
2. Add the garlic and spices and stir for 1 minute.
3. Add the tomatoes, beans and broth. Simmer 90 minutes, stirring now and then.

Variations:
- Chili over rice
- Chili over a baked potato
