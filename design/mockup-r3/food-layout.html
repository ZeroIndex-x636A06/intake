Simpler food layout: three options

Nothing here is built. Same data as today (calories, macros, allergens, plan, recipes, drinks); the question is how much of it sits on the first screen. Every option adds the split bar you liked: one bar divided protein / carbs / fat, with each share of calories under it, next to the numbers we already show.

Option 1 · Day at a glance (closest to Samsung Health)

One number, one bar, then the four meals. Each meal is a row with a plus. Allergens shrink to one chip. Plan, recipes, eating out and the extra nutrients move behind a single “More” row, so they are one tap away and not on the first screen.

The day is a list of meals with their foods showing, each with its own split bar and steppers, like the Samsung meal page but for the whole day. Totals sit in a slim strip at the top. Best if you mostly adjust what you already logged; longest to scroll.
