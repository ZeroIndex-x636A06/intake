Intake, calmer

Four tabs, a four-card Today, one Food screen, one home per feature, one Settings, and a search that finds anything. Every feature is still here. Tap the pill, the search icon and the avatar. Nothing in this page is built yet, and it has not been seen on a real phone.

Things to try

  • Slide across the pill to change tabs. The highlight follows your finger, as in the app.
  • On Today, tap Weigh in, the water gear, the planned dinner's Log in Next up, or Edit Today to hide a card.
  • Tap the search icon and type "water", "tdee", "bench" or "milk".
  • Tap the avatar for You: goals, six settings groups, help.
  • On Food, try the four sections, the ⋯ menu, and the red allergen strip.
  • On Train, tap Start, then the info button on the exercise, then Change equipment.

The pill hides on the active workout, as it does in the app. On You, no tab is lit, because You is not a tab.
