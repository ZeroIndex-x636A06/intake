Intake 2.0 · design direction

Everything you track, A to Z. One calm place.

Food, drinks, training, body, sleep and recovery, rebuilt around one log button and four clear tabs. The rules stay the same: no account, works offline, and AI is never required.

Navigation model
Today Food Train Insights

Four tabs plus a centre + that logs anything. Your avatar (top right of every tab) opens You, profile and Settings. A live pill floats above the dock while a workout runs.

One + for every log

Food, drinks, water, weight, scan, photo estimate, quick calories, a workout, measurements and progress photos all start from the same sheet instead of eight scattered buttons.

Food, without a "More" row

Nutrition becomes Food with four segments: Diary, Plan, Recipes and Library. Templates, shopping, eating out, menus and drinks now sit one tap from the tab.

Allergen safety, always in view

A shield in the Food header shows what you avoid. Every food, recipe, plan and nearby option carries one consistent chip. Allergens stay out of Settings.

Progress becomes Insights

Led by the weekly check-in and its suggestions, then a bento of trends: weight, sleep, steps, heart rate, adherence, measurements, photos and maintenance.

Today as a bento

A dark hero with calories, macros and water at a glance, a "Next up" timeline (meal, coach focus, workout), then weight, activity and meals as tiles.

Settings in four sections

The same twelve groups, sorted into Display and you, Tracking, Connections and data, and Help. Search still jumps straight to any control.

AI is labelled, never assumed

Every AI action wears the same violet AI chip and only runs on a tap or a switch you turned on. The app is complete without it.

Train keeps what works

Workout, Exercises and Videos stay as segments. Programs is promoted to a card and a header action, and the live workout pill follows you everywhere.
